Product shelf

Look for these basic product types. You don't need all of them, and simple is usually enough.

1. Gentle cleanserFragrance-free, mild, non-scrubbing. Use lukewarm water; don't wash repeatedly.
2. MoisturizerFragrance-free and non-comedogenic if you tend to break out. A light lotion may feel comfortable for oily skin; a cream may suit dry skin.
3. SunscreenBroad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher. Choose one you like wearing and reapply as directed on the label.
Optional: acne careIf breakouts bother you, ask a parent/guardian, pharmacist, or clinician about a suitable acne product. Start one new product at a time and follow its label.

Patch-test new products, stop if they cause burning or a rash, and avoid harsh scrubs, strong DIY treatments, or using lots of new products at once.

When to get help

If you have painful, persistent, worsening, or scarring acne, or a rash that doesn't settle, a healthcare professional or dermatologist can help. Sudden facial swelling, especially with trouble breathing, needs urgent medical help.
